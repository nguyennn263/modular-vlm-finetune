\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Early VQA systems treated the task as classification over a fixed answer set,
combining visual features with recurrent or attention-based language models.
Transformer-based multimodal models then became the dominant paradigm, and
generative VLMs now produce free-form answers with an autoregressive language
model~\cite{li2023blip2,alayrac2022flamingo,liu2023visualinstructiontuning}.

\paragraph{Vietnamese VQA.}
Vietnamese VQA resources include ViVQA~\cite{tran2021vivqa}, the open-ended
OpenViVQA~\cite{nguyen2023openvivqa}, and specialised datasets for synthetic
reasoning (ViCLEVR~\cite{tran2023viclevr}), book covers
(ViOCRVQA~\cite{pham2024viocrvqa}) and scene text
(ViTextVQA~\cite{nguyen2025vitextvqa}). AutoViVQA~\cite{autovivqa2026} provides
37{,}077 LLM-generated and ensemble-filtered questions on 19{,}411 COCO
images~\cite{lin2015coco}, each with five short answers and a reasoning label,
and emphasises relational, causal and multi-step reasoning. On the modelling
side, BARTPhoBEiT~\cite{tran2023bartphobeitpretrainedsequencetosequenceimage} and
AViVQA-TranConI~\cite{nguyen2024tranconi} combine visual and Vietnamese language
representations, Vintern-1B~\cite{doan2024vintern1b} brings an
instruction-tuned VLM to Vietnamese, and ViMoE-VQA~\cite{vimoevqa} introduces sparse
expert routing for generative VQA on AutoViVQA. These approaches either train a
new model or fine-tune an existing VLM on multi-tile inputs. We instead keep the
backbones of Vintern-1B frozen, replace its projector and use a single image view.

\paragraph{Frozen backbones and vision--language connectors.}
Many VLMs keep a pre-trained image encoder and language model frozen and train
only a connector between them: Frozen~\cite{tsimpoukelli2021frozen} learns a visual
prefix, BLIP-2~\cite{li2023blip2} a Q-Former, and
LLaVA~\cite{liu2023visualinstructiontuning} an MLP projector.
Honeybee~\cite{cha2024honeybee} argues for locality-preserving abstractors, and
work on inference-optimal VLMs~\cite{li2024inferenceoptimal} finds that very few
visual tokens can suffice. We compare five connectors of increasing capacity,
from a single token to a 16-query Q-Former, under the same frozen Vietnamese
backbone.

\paragraph{Parameter-efficient adaptation.}
Adapters~\cite{houlsby2019adapters}, prefix tuning~\cite{li2021prefix} and
LoRA~\cite{hu2022lora} adapt a frozen network by training a small number of
additional weights. Most work reports the gain of such methods on a fixed model.
We combine a decoder LoRA with a trainable bridge and compare the contribution of
each component, which shows where a small parameter budget is best spent in a
frozen VLM.

\paragraph{Language priors in VQA.}
VQA models are known to exploit statistical regularities between questions and
answers instead of the image; VQA v2 was built with complementary image pairs to
reduce this effect~\cite{goyal2017makingvqa}. Benchmarks whose questions and answers
are generated by LLMs from captions, such as AutoViVQA, may carry similar priors.
We therefore complement standard metrics with a question-only baseline and with
probes that measure how much the adapted model depends on the image.
