\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \textbf{ViBridge-VQA}, which improves Vintern-1B for generative Vietnamese VQA
without updating its pre-trained weights. For a VLM whose projector is aligned with its decoder,
our results suggest that, in our setup, reusing the projector with a few pooled local tokens is
more effective than learning a new connector from limited data. The visual-token budget can be
selected with an explicit accuracy--cost criterion, and local tokens complement decoder adaptation:
ViBridge-VQA with LoRA achieves the best result.

An existing Vietnamese VLM can be improved by selecting which visual tokens its frozen decoder
receives. Code and the image-disjoint split are available at
\url{https://github.com/nguyennn263/modular-vlm-finetune}.

Future work will evaluate ViBridge-VQA on additional datasets and higher-resolution views.
