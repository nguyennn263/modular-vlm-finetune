\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \textbf{ViBridge-VQA}, which improves the Vietnamese VLM Vintern-1B for generative
VQA without updating any of its pre-trained weights. For a VLM whose projector is already aligned
with its decoder, our results support three findings. First, reusing this projector with a few
pooled local tokens is more effective than learning a new connector from limited data. Second, the
number of visual tokens can be chosen with an explicit accuracy--cost criterion. Third, local
tokens complement decoder adaptation rather than replace it: ViBridge-VQA with a decoder LoRA gives
the best result.

Our study has limitations. We train on a single dataset, AutoViVQA, where the task-specific
ViMoE-VQA still reaches a higher F1, and the published baselines were obtained on the original
split rather than ours. Without decoder adaptation, the exact-match accuracy of ViBridge-VQA stays
below that of the fine-tuned Vintern-1B, and accuracy has not saturated at the 144 local tokens
available at our resolution.

Overall, an existing Vietnamese VLM can be improved substantially by choosing which visual tokens
its frozen decoder receives, and how many. The code and the image-disjoint split are available at
\url{https://github.com/nguyennn263/modular-vlm-finetune}.
