\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this work, we introduced \textbf{ViBridge-VQA}, which improves the Vietnamese VLM Vintern-1B
for generative VQA without updating any of its pre-trained weights. Our results show that, for a
VLM whose projector is already aligned with its decoder, reusing this projector with a small
number of pooled local tokens is more effective than learning a new connector from limited data,
that the number of tokens can be chosen with an explicit accuracy--cost criterion, and that the
local tokens complement decoder adaptation rather than replace it.

Our study also has limitations. We train on a single dataset, AutoViVQA, and the task-specific
ViMoE-VQA still reaches a higher F1. Without decoder adaptation, the exact-match accuracy of
ViBridge-VQA stays below that of the fine-tuned Vintern-1B, and with 50 visual tokens it reads
scene text much worse than Vintern-1B; accuracy had not saturated at the 144 local tokens
available at our resolution.

Overall, an existing Vietnamese VLM can be improved substantially by choosing which visual tokens
its frozen decoder receives, and how many. The code and the image-disjoint split are available at
\url{https://github.com/nguyennn263/modular-vlm-finetune}.
