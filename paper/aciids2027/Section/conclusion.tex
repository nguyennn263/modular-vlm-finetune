\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this work, we introduced \textbf{ViBridge-VQA}, which improves the Vietnamese VLM Vintern-1B
for generative VQA without updating any of its pre-trained weights. Our results support three
findings for a VLM whose projector is already aligned with its decoder. First, reusing this
projector with a few pooled local tokens is more effective than learning a new connector from
limited data. Second, the number of tokens can be chosen with an explicit accuracy--cost
criterion. Third, the local tokens complement decoder adaptation rather than replace it.

Our study also has limitations. We train on a single dataset, AutoViVQA, and the task-specific ViMoE-VQA still reaches a higher F1. The comparison with published results
is also limited, as they were obtained on the original AutoViVQA split. Without decoder adaptation, the exact-match accuracy of
ViBridge-VQA stays below that of the fine-tuned Vintern-1B. With 50 visual tokens, it also reads scene text much worse than Vintern-1B, a gap that closes
with all 144 local tokens. Finally, accuracy has not saturated at the 144 local tokens available at our resolution.

Overall, an existing Vietnamese VLM can be improved substantially by choosing which visual tokens
its frozen decoder receives, and how many. The code and the image-disjoint split are available at
\url{https://github.com/nguyennn263/modular-vlm-finetune}.
